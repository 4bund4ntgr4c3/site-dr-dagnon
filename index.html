Profile

A career serving the health of African populations

A physician and public health specialist, Dr. Seynudé Jean-Fortuné Dagnon brings over 17 years of experience designing, managing, implementing, monitoring and evaluating programs against malaria, HIV/AIDS, neglected tropical diseases, outbreak response and maternal, newborn and child health — in Benin and across Africa.

He is now Senior Program Officer — Malaria / Francophone Africa at the Gates Foundation, where his work focuses on malaria implementation strategies, grant and investment management, partner engagement, data-driven decision-making and support to national malaria control programs.
