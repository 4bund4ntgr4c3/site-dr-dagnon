EXECUTIVE PROFILE
Physician and global health leader with 20 years across clinical medicine, research, public health, donor programming and philanthropy, including more than a decade leading malaria and health-system programs and investments across Africa.
As Senior Program Officer for Malaria at the Gates Foundation, directly manages a portfolio of US$39.4M in cumulative multi-year commitments across selected investments since 2020 and provides technical oversight of a further US$122 million in grants across 15 countries, setting regional investment priorities in direct engagement with ministries of health, national malaria control programs, donors and multilateral partners.
Combines rigorous financial management and negotiation with a record of mobilizing resources at continental scale, including support to the initiative that tripled domestic government financing for malaria between 2020 and 2025 in Benin and internal advocacy that expanded the Foundation’s allocation to Francophone Africa. Translates scientific evidence into investment cases, policy recommendations and operating models that accelerate the introduction, financing and scale-up of health innovations, including digital health and AI-enabled surveillance and decision-support tools.
Operates at the intersection of science, government and philanthropy, with engagement extending to Heads of State and Government. Has led through uncertainty, directing multi-country responses to Ebola preparedness, Lassa fever and COVID-19 alongside a core malaria mandate. Brings clinical credibility, government relationships across Francophone and Anglophone Africa and a peer-reviewed publication record to executive decision-making.
SELECTED LEADERSHIP IMPACT

Leadership under uncertainty (2015–2020) — Directed the U.S. Mission’s multi-disease emergency response — Ebola preparedness, Lassa fever and COVID-19 — in support of the Government of Benin, sustaining essential malaria services through each period of disruption.
